NetRadio Support

NetRadio is an internet radio and podcast player for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It’s free, with no ads and no account.

Contact

Send questions, bug reports and ideas to 20netradioapp26@gmail.com. Please mention your device, system version and NetRadio version (shown at the bottom of Help).

Frequently asked questions

A station doesn’t play or shows Connection error.
The station’s stream may be temporarily down. Try playing it again, or search for another address of the same station.

The song title is missing.
The station doesn’t broadcast it. NetRadio tries several sources; there is nothing more it can do.

A station has no logo.
Neither the station directory nor the station’s website has one. NetRadio tries again after 7 days.

How do I move my presets to another device?
Open Backups in the footer, choose Save to file and send the file, for example via AirDrop or iCloud Drive. On the other device choose Load from file…

Why can’t I play Spotify podcasts or Apple Podcasts subscriptions?
These podcasts have no public RSS feed. NetRadio plays podcasts with a public feed and private RSS links of paid podcasts.

Podcast search says it’s limited.
The Apple Podcasts directory has temporarily limited the number of searches. Try again in a minute.

How do I change the app’s language?
iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apps → NetRadio → Language. Mac: System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.

Does NetRadio store my data?
No. Presets, subscriptions and settings stay only on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26, iPad with iPadOS 26 or Mac with macOS 26 or later. An internet connection is required.
