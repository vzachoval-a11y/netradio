Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

NetRadio doesn’t collect any personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer receives no data from you.

Who is responsible

NetRadio is developed by Vladimír Zachoval. Contact: 20netradioapp26@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Tabs and station presets, podcast subscriptions, listening positions, backups and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere and don’t sync between devices on their own. Deleting the app deletes them. A backup file is created only when you save one yourself, and you decide where it goes.

Services the app connects to

To play radio and podcasts, NetRadio connects to these services. Like any website, they see your IP address, but NetRadio sends them no name, email or device identifier.

  • radio-browser.info station directory: receives your search text and, when a station starts, an anonymous record used to rank stations by popularity.
  • Apple Podcasts directory (iTunes Search API): receives only your search text and the selected country.
  • Radio stations and podcast publishers: audio plays directly from their servers. Logos and artwork are loaded from their websites in the same way.
  • Czech Radio (Český rozhlas): for its stations, the programme and song names are loaded from its public interface.

These services have their own privacy policies.

Children

NetRadio doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to export or delete. You delete the data on your device by removing the app. For questions, use the contact above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
