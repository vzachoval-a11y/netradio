User guide

Download the guide as PDF

NetRadio plays internet radio stations from around the world and podcasts on iPhone, iPad and Mac. A single tap on a preset starts a station; everything else is one step away.

What’s on the screen

  • Header: logo, name and playback status. On the Mac there is also a pin that keeps the window above other windows.
  • Radio | Podcasts switch: switches between radio and podcasts. Playback continues when you switch, and only one thing plays at a time.
  • Now Playing: station logo and name, country and stream quality, programme, song and artist, the three previous songs and volume.
  • Presets: up to 5 tabs of 10 stations each.
  • Floating bar at the bottom: what’s playing, with play / pause and stop. For podcasts, ±15 seconds buttons replace stop.
  • Footer: Backups, Help, Settings and, on the Mac, Quit radio.

A short guide is also built into the app: the Help link in the footer, on the Mac the Help menu or ⌘?.

Listening to radio

  • Tap a preset and the station starts playing.
  • Pause and resume from the bar at the bottom. After a pause the radio reconnects to the live broadcast.
  • Set the volume with the slider in the Now Playing panel.
  • Radio keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked. You can control it from the Lock Screen, Control Center, headphones and the Mac’s media keys.

Playback-only mode

  • When a station starts playing, the presets hide and an enlarged Now Playing panel with the bar remains.
  • Bring the presets back with the grid button in the bar (on the Mac also ⌥⌘P).
  • After pausing, stopping or a connection error the presets come back on their own.

Mini player (Mac)

  • The mini player button in the bar, the Controls menu or ⌥⌘M shrinks the player into a small window that floats above all windows.
  • Drag the window anywhere outside its buttons and sliders. The space bar plays and pauses.
  • The same button or ⌥⌘M again takes you back to the main window.

Presets and tabs

  • Tabs: up to 5 tabs of 10 stations. Add one with + New tab.
  • Tab menu (tap the open tab): Rename, Move left / right, Empty, Delete.
  • Preset menu (••• button, long press or right-click): Play, Rename, Replace with another station, Move, Move to tab, Remove.
  • Empty preset: tap it to open search; the chosen station is saved straight into that slot.

Finding stations

  • Type a station name or a genre, for example “country”.
  • Choose a country and a genre. Without any text you see the most popular stations of the chosen country.
  • The ▶ button tries a station, + Save adds it to your presets.

Podcasts

  • Find a podcast searches the Apple Podcasts directory by title or author. Without text it shows the top podcasts chart.
  • Add by link (chain icon) accepts an RSS feed address or an Apple Podcasts link.
  • Follow adds a podcast to your followed podcasts. The number on a podcast shows new episodes.
  • Episode list: a dot marks a new episode, Played a finished one, a bar one in progress. Long-press an episode to mark it played or play it from the start.
  • Playback: a position slider, speed from 0.8× to 2× and ±15 seconds in the bar. The app remembers where you stopped.
  • Podcasts aren’t downloaded; they stream over the internet.

Backups and moving to another device

  1. Open Backups in the footer (on the Mac also ⇧⌘B).
  2. Save stores all tabs, stations and followed podcasts under a name.
  3. Restore brings back a saved set. The current state is backed up automatically first.
  4. Save to file and Load from file… move everything to another device.

Settings and language

  • Open Settings from the footer, on the Mac also with ⌘,. The option Close the episode list when an episode starts decides whether the list closes on its own.
  • NetRadio is available in Czech, Slovak, Polish, English, German, French and Italian and follows the system language.
  • To change the language for NetRadio only: on iPhone and iPad Settings → Apps → NetRadio → Language; on the Mac System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.
